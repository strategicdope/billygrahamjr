About BG

20 years in uniform. Still serving.

Billy Graham Jr. is a global speaker, coach, trainer, and retired Air Force veteran. After serving 20 years in the military, he now develops leaders and teams.

He coaches with transparency and conviction, letting people see into his life so they'll reflect on their own. He challenges you to demand more from yourself and hold yourself to a higher standard.

That work has been recognized around the world with the iChange Nations™ Global Inspiration Award and the Kavod Foundation International Service Award.
